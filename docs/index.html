A stupid name for a really good browser.

It blocks ads, puts idle tabs to sleep and keeps your history on your own computer. It’s free, there’s no account, and it works on Mac, Windows and Android.

It’s watching. Click it.
The new tab: weather, to-dos and notes you can drag anywhere.

Half the memory, same tabs.

Tabs you haven’t looked at in a while fall asleep. They stay where they are and wake up the moment you click them, while your computer gets its memory back.

Measured on The Stupid Browser with five tabs: 1,577 MB awake, 738 MB with four asleep. Idle CPU dropped from 2.1% to 0.1%.

Everything else you’d want, and nothing you wouldn’t.

Shield

Ads and trackers are blocked before they load, so pages are lighter and nobody follows you from site to site.

Buddy

A little AI hiker that can read the tab you’re on. Highlight any text and ask about it. It runs on Claude with your own key.

Rewind

Find that page from Tuesday by what it looked like or said. It’s off until you turn it on, and it stays on your computer.

Make it yours

Widgets, backgrounds, your own quotes on the new tab, any accent color, and Arc-style vertical tabs if that’s your thing.

Bring your stuff

Move your bookmarks, history and passwords over from the browser you use now. Nothing changes in the old one.

Music island

When music plays in a tab you’ve left, a small pill floats up with play, pause and skip. Click it to jump back.

And the small stuff

Tab groups, split view, pinned tabs and reopening closed ones. WhatsApp, Discord and Spotify in the sidebar. Private windows, a password manager, separate profiles, DuckDuckGo by default and 13 other search engines, a sound equalizer for headphones, and updates that install themselves.

Get it. It’s free.

Pick your computer or phone. No sign-up, no email, no catch.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer

Apple chip M1, M2, M3, M4 Intel Older Macs

Not sure? Open the Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip, pick Apple chip.

First time opening it

Drag it into Applications and open it. macOS asks once because the app isn’t from the App Store: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

If it says the app is damaged, open Terminal and run these two lines:

xattr -cr "/Applications/The Stupid Browser.app"
codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/The Stupid Browser.app"

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Installer Recommended Portable No install, runs from a USB stick

If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Android

Android 8 or newer, about 1.5 MB

Download the app .apk

Open the file on your phone. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser, which is normal for apps outside Google Play.
